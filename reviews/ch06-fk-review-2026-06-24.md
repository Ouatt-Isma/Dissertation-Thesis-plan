## 0. The three messages behind the 73 comments

1. **The chapter lacks a guide-rail.** (#31, #29, #46, #64, #62, #24) Frank cannot tell what the framework is, what the contributions are, and what each of the three applications is *for* (teaching the method? illustrating? evaluating?). Every application needs an explicit "why this dataset / what we want to show / research question" paragraph and a closing "what this showed" paragraph.
2. **Methodology and examples are interleaved.** (#25, #26, #27, #31, #34, #35) Phase I/II boundary is unclear; §6.4.1 (metric→q→evidence) is methodology but sits inside the COMPAS section; the BPQ/EWQ/CUQ formulas duplicate §3.3.2. Proposal: all *structure* (tree, trust sources, Table 6.1, Fig 6.1) in Phase I; all *quantification* (q-normalisation, K_m, BPQ/EWQ/CUQ recap) in Phase II; applications only *instantiate*.
3. **Table 6.1 / the trust-source catalogue is underexplained and is the root of most confusion downstream.** (#18, #22, #24, #37, #42, #72, #73) The metrics used in COMPAS are never defined, "reliability" is never scoped (why is PII density evidence about *reliability*?), and the table is banished to the chapter end.

Plus two cross-cutting terminology decisions: **trust source vs. metric** (#9, #19, #32, #33) and **trust/distrust mass vs. belief/disbelief, t vs. b** (#39, #68 — and Alg. 4 / Eq. 6.19 already use *b*, so the chapter is internally inconsistent; `ptas.tex` uses "trust mass" 35×, `methodology.tex` uses belief/disbelief → thesis-wide decision).
