## Major

### 1. Convergence theorem — done
- Group assumption removed; §7.7.1 now explains why no SL fusion operator forms a group and states the update as three maps per batch.
- Theorem 7.1 restated as *Stability of the Parameter-Trust Update*: (i) operator-specific limits for constant q (ABF/WBF → q; CBF → dogmatic, u ~ 1/n; BCF → vertex or ω₀-fixed ratio; vacuous q: ABF → vacuous, others keep ω₀); (ii) implemented update under a fully trusted conditioning factor: uncertainty contracts to u* = u_q/15, disbelief is an EMA of batch evidence (window 16), so with fluctuating mini-batch evidence the opinion is a running mean, not a limit; (iii) multiplicative revision with fixed base rate.
- Appendix A rewritten: Thm A.1 (ABF/WBF, reciprocal-uncertainty contraction, explicit factors — fixes "decreasing distance ⇒ limit q"), A.2 (CBF via evidence accumulation), A.3 (BCF via plausibility ratios; remark with the (0.4,0.35,0.25) amplification and the float drift + renormalisation), A.4 (implemented update, fixed point verified numerically against the released code), A.5 (multiplication: b_n → 0 derived from d_n → 1, no approximation; fixed base rate a = 1/2 as implemented).
- **Finding while re-checking the implementation:** the code applies a third sub-step not in the old Algorithm 6, `T_θ ← T_θ ⊖ (T_θ ⊙ T_lr)` with T_lr = (1,0,0). With base rate ½, ⊙(1,0,0) is *not* the identity ((b,d,u) ↦ (b+u/3, d, 2u/3)), so the two multiplication sub-steps contract the uncertainty every batch. This is what makes the operator ablation look flat under trusted data. Algorithm 6 and §7.6 now document the sub-step.
- Parameter-uncertainty semantics per operator: new paragraph after Theorem 7.1.
- Fusion ablation: the old "agree within 0.002" came from runs where CBF/WBF consumed a gradient stream *regenerated by continued training from the cached model* (`start_client` replays `nn.train` on the loaded model). Re-run with fresh training per operator: cancer TM (trust/trust) ABF 0.859±0.027, WBF 0.861±0.026, CBF 0.755±0.079, BCF 0.601±0.425; CBF/BCF make every first-layer opinion dogmatic. New Table `tab:fusion_ops` with cancer rows (3 seeds) and MNIST 784-128-10 rows (1 seed). MNIST nuance: under trusted data all four operators agree within 0.015 (0.988/0.988/0.976/0.991) because with 785 incoming edges the fixed-point uncertainty u_q/15 ≈ 2e-4 is dogmatic for every operator; under vacuous data they spread 0.335/0.347/0.392/0.421. So the old "immaterial" claim is a wide-input-layer, trusted-data property, not a general one — the text says so.
