\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Meaning / Description} \\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Notational Conventions}} \\
$\omega$ vs.\ $T$ & $\omega$ denotes a general subjective opinion; $T$ denotes an opinion specifically interpreted as \emph{trust}. \\
$\mathbf{W}, \mathbf{b}$ vs.\ $W, b$ & Bold symbols denote \acs{NN} weight matrices / bias vectors; non-bold denotes the \acs{SL} prior weight $W$ and belief mass $b_x$ respectively. \\
$P$ (property vs.\ PNPS) & Two distinct uses, disambiguated by context: a trustworthiness property $P$ in all chapters except 4, and a Parallel Non-intersecting Path Subnetwork (PNPS) in Ch.~4 only. \\
$\omega = (t,d,u)$ vs.\ $(b,d,u,a)$ & The Actual Trust Level (ATL) is written as a triple $\omega=(t,d,u)=(b,d,u)$; the base rate $a$ is left implicit (default $a=0.5$ unless stated otherwise). The full binomial opinion retains $a$ explicitly as $(b,d,u,a)$. \\
$\gamma$ & Two distinct uses depending on context: the limit weights $\gamma_X^A, \gamma_X^B$ in \acs{SL} fusion operators vs.\ the normalization factor in Constant-Uncertainty Quantification. Always disambiguated by accompanying sub/superscripts or surrounding context. \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Sets and Spaces}} \\
$\Omega$ & Universe (frame of discernment) \\
$\mathbb{X}$ & Opinion state space / domain \\
$\mathcal{R}(\mathbb{X})$ & Hyperdomain of $\mathbb{X}$ \\
$\Delta^{K-1}$ & $(K{-}1)$-dimensional probability simplex \\
$K$ & Cardinality of a domain (e.g.\ number of classes; in \acs{NN} classification, equivalently the number of output neurons) \\
$\mathcal{D}$ & Dataset \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Probability and Evidence Theory}} \\
$\Pr(\cdot)$ & Classical / Bayesian probability \\
$Q(P)$ & Second-order probability distribution over $P$ \\
$\alpha, \beta$ & Beta distribution parameters (pseudo-counts of positive/negative evidence) \\
$\Gamma(\cdot)$ & Gamma function \\
$\boldsymbol{\alpha} = (\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_K)$ & Dirichlet concentration vector \\
$\alpha_0$ & Sum of Dirichlet concentration parameters, $\sum_i \alpha_i$ \\
$m(\cdot)$ & Basic Belief Assignment (BBA), DST \\
$\mathrm{Con}$ & Conflict mass between two belief sources (Dempster's rule \& BCF) \\
$r, s$ & Positive/negative evidence counts supporting a proposition (shared meaning: Beta--opinion mapping) \\
$W$ & Non-informative prior weight (Beta/Dirichlet $\leftrightarrow$ opinion mapping) \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Subjective Opinions}} \\
$\omega_x = (b_x, d_x, u_x, a_x)$ & Binomial opinion: belief, disbelief, uncertainty, base rate \\
$\omega_X = (\mathbf{b}, u, \mathbf{a})$ & Multinomial opinion \\
$\omega_X^A$ & Opinion held by agent $A$ about variable $X$ \\
$\mathbb{P}[\omega]$ & Projected probability of opinion $\omega$ (Eq.~2.2) \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{\acs{SL} Operators}} \\
$(\cdot)$ & Binomial opinion multiplication (logical conjunction, Def.~2.7) \\
$\odot$ & Belief constraint fusion (BCF) \\
$\oplus$ & Cumulative belief fusion (CBF) \\
$\overline{\oplus}$ & Averaging belief fusion (ABF) \\
$\hat{\oplus}$ & Weighted belief fusion (WBF) \\
$\occ$ & Consensus \& compromise fusion (CCF) \\
$\circledcirc$ & Inferential deduction operator \\
$\circleddash$ & Trust-revision operator \\
$\oslash$ & Conservative trust-division operator \\
$\otimes_{TE}$ & Two-edge path trust discounting \\
$\otimes_{ME}$ & Multi-edge path trust discounting \\
$\otimes_{RE}$ & Referral-edge path trust discounting \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Graph Structure}} \\
$v$ & Node (vertex) \\
$e$ & Edge \\
$G = (V,E)$ & Directed graph with vertex set $V$ and edge set $E$ \\
$\mathcal{N}(i)$ & Set of incoming edges to neuron $i$ \\
$P$ & PNPS \\
$\mathcal{P}_G$ & Set of all PNPSs of graph $G$ \\
$\mathrm{NL}_G(e)$ & Nesting Level of edge $e$ in $G$ \\
$\mathrm{NNL}_G(v)$ & Node Nesting Level of $v$ in $G$ \\
$\mathrm{PNL}_G(P)$ & Nesting Level of PNPS $P$: $\min\{\mathrm{NL}_G(e) \mid e \text{ is an edge of } P\}$ \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Dataset Trust Design Space}} \\
$S$ & Lifecycle stage being assessed, $S \in \{\text{SAMPLING, COLLECTION, ANNOTATION, PROCESSING}\}$ \\
$G$ & Granularity at which a property is assessed, $G \in \{\text{DATASET, ENTRY, FEATURE}\}$ \\
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Dataset Trust-Source Quantification (Ch.~6.4.1)}} \\
$m$ & Index/identifier of a trust-source metric \\
$q_m$ & Normalized quality score of metric $m$, $q_m \in [0,1]$ \\
$\tau_m$ & Tolerance threshold for lower-is-better metrics (Eq.~6.6) \\
$A_m, L_m$ & Unacceptable / ideal bounds for higher-is-better metrics (Eq.~6.7) \\
$K_m$ & Evidence-strength parameter of metric $m$ (total evidence contributed, $r_m+s_m=K_m$) \\
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Neural Network Notation (Ch.~2.3)}} \\
$\Theta = (\mathbf{W}, \mathbf{b}, \phi)$ & Full \acs{NN} parameterization \\
$\mathbf{W} = \{\mathbf{W}^{(l)}\}$ & Weight matrices \\
$\mathbf{b} = \{\mathbf{b}^{(l)}\}$ & Bias vectors \\
$\phi$ & Activation functions \\
$L$ & Total number of layers \\
$n_l$ & Number of neurons in layer $l$ \\
$\mathcal{L}$ & Loss function \\
$lr$ & Learning rate \\
$\delta_i^{(l)}$ & Error term of neuron $i$ in layer $l$ \\
$\hat{y}(x)$ & Predicted class \\
$N$ & Number of samples in the training dataset $\mathcal{D}$ \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Trust Opinion Quantification (Ch.~3.3.2)}} \\
$w_x$ & Evidence weight in Evidence-Weighted Quantification (EWQ, Eq.~3.2) \\
$U$ & Fixed target uncertainty level in Constant-Uncertainty Quantification (CUQ, Eq.~3.3) \\
$\gamma$ & Normalization factor in CUQ, $\gamma = \dfrac{1-U}{r_x+s_x}$ (Eq.~3.3) \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Calibration (Ch.~2.3.2)}} \\
$\hat{p}(x)$ & Predicted confidence for input $x$ \\
$M$ & Number of confidence bins \\
$B_m$ & Set of sample indices whose predicted confidence falls in bin $m$ \\
$\mathrm{acc}(B_m)$ & Empirical accuracy of samples in bin $B_m$ \\
$\mathrm{conf}(B_m)$ & Average predicted confidence of samples in bin $B_m$ \\
$n$ & Total number of samples used for calibration evaluation$^{*}$ \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Model and Parameters}} \\

$l$ & Layer index \\
$n_i^{(l)}$ & Neuron $i$ in layer $l$ \\
$\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}$ & Model parameter connecting neuron $j$ in layer $l{-}1$ to neuron $i$ in layer $l$ \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Algorithm Inputs}} \\
$g$ & Collection of gradients for all parameters in the batch \\
$T_y$ & Trust opinion on label $y$ \\
$\epsilon$ & Gradient sensitivity threshold in \textsc{NodeTrust} \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Batch and Gradient Quantities}} \\
$T_{y_{\text{batch}}}$ & Aggregated trust over labels in the current batch \\
$g^{(l)}_{i,j}$ & Gradient of loss w.r.t.\ $\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}$ \\
$g^{(l)}_{i}$ & Gradient vector for neuron $n_i^{(l)}$ \\
$r$ & Count of weak gradients: $|g^{(l)}_{i,j}| < \epsilon$ \\
$s$ & Count of strong gradients: $|g^{(l)}_{i,j}| \ge \epsilon$ \\[4pt]
\hline
\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Trust Values}} \\
$T_{n_i \mid y_{\text{batch}}}$ & Trust in neuron $n_i$ conditioned on batch labels \\
$T_{n_i \mid \overline{y_{\text{batch}}}}$ & Trust in neuron under incorrect labels (vacuous) \\
$T_{n_i \parallel Y_{\text{batch}}}$ & Deduced trust in neuron $n_i$ \\
$T_{\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}}$ & Trust opinion on parameter $\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}$ \\
$T_{x^{(l-1)}_j}$ & Trust in the input to parameter $\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}$ \\

$T_D$ & Trust assessment of the training dataset $D$ \\
$T_x$ & Trust assessment of the input $x$ \\

$T_{y'}$ & Trust opinion on the model output / prediction $y'$ \\
$Pf_\Theta$ & The parallel trust function: $Pf_\Theta(T_x) = T(f_\Theta(x))$ \\
