$g^{(l)}_{i}$ & Gradient vector for neuron $n_i^{(l)}$ \\
$r$ & Count of weak gradients: $|g^{(l)}_{i,j}| < \epsilon$ \\

\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Trust Values}} \\
$T_{n_i \mid y_{\text{batch}}}$ & Trust in neuron $n_i$ conditioned on batch labels \\
$T_{n_i \mid \overline{y_{\text{batch}}}}$ & Trust in neuron under incorrect labels (vacuous) \\
$T_{n_i \parallel Y_{\text{batch}}}$ & Deduced trust in neuron $n_i$ \\
$T_{\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}}$ & Trust opinion on parameter $\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}$ \\
$T_{x^{(l-1)}_j}$ & Trust in the input to parameter $\theta^{(l)}_{i,j}$ \\[4pt]

\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Operators}} \\
$\bigwedge$ ($\bigoplus$) & Fusion of trust opinions \\
$\circledcirc$ & Inferential deduction operator \\
$\circleddash$ & Trust-revision operator \\
$\odot$ & SL binomial multiplication / constraint fusion:
         $\omega_X^{A\&B} = \omega_X^A \odot \omega_X^B$ \\
$\oslash$ & Conservative trust-division operator \\[4pt]
